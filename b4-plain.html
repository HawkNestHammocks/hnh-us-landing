What it actually is

A hammock and a tent in one. Bug net sewn in, rain fly over the top, waterproof floor underneath, and a pocket that takes a sleeping pad up to 25 inches wide so your back isn’t cold.

A regular hammock isn’t the same thing. No net, no cover, and below about 50°F the air moving underneath makes it a wind tunnel. That’s a nap, not a night’s sleep.

The part people get wrong

Everyone worries about rain. Rain is the easy part — that’s just fabric. Cold is what ruins a night in a hammock, and it comes from underneath, where your sleeping bag is squashed flat and does nothing.

That’s what the pad pocket is for. Your pad sits under your back, held in place, instead of sliding off at 2am like it does in a normal hammock.
