The daily loop

Two minutes
a day.

The check-in is the whole system. It's the part that moves the needle, and it's over before your coffee cools.

  1. 1

    Four cards, that's it

    A drill, a real-world task, a quick status check, and one rotating card — water, gear, a skill, a scenario. Never the same four days running.

  2. 2

    Answer honestly

    Nothing here is graded on knowing the answer. It's graded on doing the thing. Say you don't have it and the app builds you a plan to get it.

  3. 3

    Watch the dial move

    Points land the second you finish. You'll see exactly which pillar went up and by how much.

Up to +40 a day across the four pillars. Skip it and the number sits still — then starts sliding.
