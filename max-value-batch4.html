Chapter One

The Problem Nobody
Talks About

You've hiked all day. You reach camp, pitch on the flattest ground you can find — which isn't flat — and crawl in. A root finds your hip. The cold comes up through the floor. By 2am you're still turning over, wondering why you thought this was fun.

Ground sleeping is the part everyone quietly tolerates. And a normal hammock isn't the fix — no bug net, no rain cover, and below about 50°F the air underneath turns it into a wind tunnel. It's a nap, not a shelter.
