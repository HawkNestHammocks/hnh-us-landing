Questions about giving a Hawk Nest

No, and we want to be upfront about that. Your Hawk Nest is being made fresh in our First Run of 2027 and ships February 1, 2027. The gift card is what arrives for Christmas, so there’s something to unwrap on the day.

When will the gift card arrive?

Order by December 14 and we mail the printed card by USPS First-Class Mail in time for Christmas (lower 48 states). Every gift order also gets an instant printable card by email, so if you order later, or the mail runs slow, you can print it at home.

Can I ship it to the person I’m giving it to?

Yes. The gift card and the hammock tent both go to the shipping address on your order. Giving it in person? Use your own address and wrap the card yourself. Want the card sent to you but the hammock tent shipped to them? Add both addresses in the gift note in your cart.

Does the gift card cost extra?

No. The gift card is free with every gift order. Just tick “This is a Christmas gift” in your cart.

Does the gift card show the price?

No. The gift card never shows a price.

What if it’s not the right fit for them?

Returns are accepted within 60 days of delivery when the hammock tent is unused or like new, in its original packaging and free of odours. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the order includes our Free Returns add-on.

Will I get tracking?

Yes. We email tracking the day your Hawk Nest ships on February 1.
