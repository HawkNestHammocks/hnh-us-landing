While you wait

Get ready for night one

Six quick tips so your first hang feels easy.

Tip 01

🌲 Pick two healthy trees

Look for two living trees about 12 to 15 ft apart, with trunks at least 6″ across and no dead branches overhead. The included tree-friendly straps wrap flat around the trunk, so they don't dig into the bark.

Tip 02

🪑 Hang it low

Aim for about chair height. It's easier to climb in and out, and you're close to the ground if you need anything. Pull the straps snug so the hammock sits nice and steady.

Tip 03

🛏️ Pad goes in the pocket

Slide your sleeping pad into the pocket on the underside. It stays put all night and blocks the cold air from below. A pad isn't included, so add one to your gear list.

Tip 04

🔥 Know your warmth

How warm it sleeps depends on your setup:

≈ −5°C(23°F) with a sleeping pad in the pocket
≈ −20°C(−4°F) extreme, with the ThermoNest underquilt + a good winter bag

Based on what campers have seen, not lab-tested. Your bag and layers matter too.

Tip 05

⛺ No trees? Ground it

Camping somewhere bare? Your Hawk Nest also pitches on the ground as a tent, using the poles and stakes in the box. One shelter for every campsite.

Tip 06

🎒 It packs small

Everything fits in the waterproof carry bag, about 16.5″ × 5.5″ and around 4.5 lb. That's easy to strap to a pack, toss in a kayak or keep in the trunk.

Questions

Quick answers

When will my Hawk Nest ship?

Orders in the First Run of 2027 are planned to ship February 1, 2027. If anything changes, we'll email the address on the order.

How will I know it has shipped?

We email a tracking number to the email on the order as soon as it ships. If it was a gift, the tracking goes to whoever placed the order.

Is this tracker for my specific order?

It follows the whole First Run of 2027 batch, which your order is part of. For anything about your own order, email support@hawknesthammocks.ca with the order number.

Need to update the shipping address?

Email support@hawknesthammocks.ca with the order number and the new address, ideally well before Feb 1.

What's in the box?

The hammock tent with built-in bug net, the rain fly, spreader and arch poles, 2 tree straps, stakes, guy lines and the waterproof carry bag. A sleeping pad isn't included. Bring your own and slide it into the underside pocket.

What can I add to stay warmer?

A sleeping pad in the underside pocket is the big one. For cold-weather trips, the ThermoNest underquilt plus a good winter sleeping bag takes it much further.
